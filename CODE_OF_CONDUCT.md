# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Help keep discussions relevant, inclusive, and safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and other abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe.
